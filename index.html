Rules: High Internal Temperature → dispatch HVAC. Zone Not Regulating + Supply Air Sensor both open on one unit → dispatch HVAC. If a store already has an HVAC truck rolling for one unit, other non-comms watch items at that store move into dispatch too — checked on the same visit instead of a separate trip. Loss of Communication sits on watch until it's been open 7+ days, then escalates to a call to EnTouch — comms loss is a panel/controller issue, not a truck roll, so it doesn't get pulled in just because a truck happens to be on-site. Multiple units losing comms at one site within 24 hours of each other is flagged as a shared-panel hint, but still waits for the 7-day threshold before escalating. Alerts idle 150+ days are flagged as stale rather than live. Checkboxes persist across sessions on this device.
